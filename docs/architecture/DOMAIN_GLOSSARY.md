# Domain Glossary

This document defines the ubiquitous language used throughout the Bible Study & Ministry Platform.

Every package should use these terms consistently.

---

# Bible Domain

## BibleCanon

A recognised collection of biblical books.

## Testament

A major division of a biblical canon.

## BibleDivision

A grouping of books within a testament.

## BibleBook

A canonical book of Scripture.

## BibleReference

A location within Scripture.

## ChapterNumber

The number identifying a chapter in a biblical book.

## VerseNumber

The number identifying a verse within a chapter.

## Translation

A textual rendering of Scripture.

## Versification

A numbering system defining chapters and verses.

---

# Study Domain

## StudyProject

A complete Bible study created by a user.

## Observation

Facts directly observed from the biblical text.

## Interpretation

The intended meaning of the text.

## Application

Practical implications for life and ministry.

---

# Sermon Domain

## Sermon

A preached exposition of Scripture.

## Proposition

The central biblical truth communicated.

## Outline

The structural organisation of the sermon.

## Illustration

A supporting example or story.

## Application Point

A practical response for listeners.

---

# Teaching Domain

## Lesson

A structured teaching session.

## Learning Objective

The intended educational outcome.

## Discussion Question

A question designed to promote interaction.

## Assignment

Work completed before or after teaching.

---

# AI Domain

## Prompt

A structured instruction sent to an AI model.

## Context

The information supplied alongside a prompt.

## Conversation

A sequence of interactions with an AI assistant.

---

# Shared Domain

## Entity

An object with a persistent identity.

## Value Object

An immutable object defined by its values.

## Repository

An abstraction for retrieving and storing domain objects.

## Domain Service

Business logic that does not naturally belong to a single entity.

## Aggregate

A consistency boundary within the domain model.

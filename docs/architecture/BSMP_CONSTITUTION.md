# BSMP Constitution

Version: 1.0

Status: Living Document

---

# Mission

To build a world-class, open, modular, extensible platform that helps people study, understand, teach, and proclaim the Word of God through carefully engineered software.

---

# Vision

BSMP exists to become a long-term platform for:

- Bible Study
- Expository Preaching
- Teaching
- Biblical Research
- Ministry Preparation
- AI-assisted learning

The platform should remain useful and maintainable for decades.

---

# Core Values

## Truth Before Convenience

The domain model must accurately represent biblical concepts.

Convenience must never distort the domain.

---

## Domain First

The domain is the heart of BSMP.

Infrastructure serves the domain.

The domain never serves infrastructure.

---

## Simplicity

Choose the simplest design that correctly models the problem.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

## Clarity

Code should communicate intent.

Prefer explicitness over cleverness.

---

## Consistency

Every package follows the same architectural principles.

Consistency is more valuable than novelty.

---

## Extensibility

Every major design decision should consider future growth.

The platform should welcome new capabilities rather than resist them.

---

## Standards

Use established industry standards whenever they improve interoperability.

Do not reinvent standards without a compelling reason.

---

## Documentation

Architecture is documented.

Decisions are documented.

Public APIs are documented.

---

## Testing

Business rules must be testable independently of frameworks.

---

## Stewardship

Every contribution should leave the codebase better than it was found.

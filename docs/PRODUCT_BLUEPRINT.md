# Bible Study Ministry Platform (BSMP)

## Motto

Study the Word. Understand the Truth. Live the Gospel.

## Mission

To equip Christians to study, understand, apply, teach, and preach God's Word through disciplined inductive Bible study enhanced by responsible AI guidance.

## North Star

Every feature we build must help someone become a more faithful student, teacher, or preacher of God's Word.


# The BSMP captures the heart of the platform perfectly. It describes a journey:

Study the Word → Observation
Understand the Truth → Interpretation
Live the Gospel → Application

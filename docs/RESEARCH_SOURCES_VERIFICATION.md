# External Research Verification

1. Sign in and open a Study.
2. Open `/research?studyId=...`.
3. Enable **Include external research**.
4. Enter a focused question.
5. Optionally add one or more complete HTTPS public URLs.
6. Run **Research with Sources**.
7. Confirm the response remains grounded in the selected Study and that retrieved source citations appear separately.
8. Open a retrieved source in a new tab and verify the URL matches the displayed citation.
9. Return to Research and verify the Study Workspace, Biblical Theology, and Teaching links still preserve the same `studyId`.
10. Confirm no Study evidence, interpretation, Biblical Theology, or sermon record was automatically changed by the external research run.

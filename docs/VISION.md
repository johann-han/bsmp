# Bible Study Ministry Platform (BSMP)


Mission

To equip Christians to study Scripture faithfully through disciplined inductive Bible study enhanced by responsible AI guidance.

North Star

Every feature we build must help someone become a more faithful student, teacher, or preacher of God's Word.

## Vision

To equip Christians, Bible students, pastors, teachers, and ministry leaders to study, understand, apply, teach, and preach God's Word through a disciplined inductive Bible study methodology enhanced by responsible artificial intelligence.

---

# Mission

BSMP exists to help people become better students of Scripture.

The goal is not merely to provide answers.

The goal is to teach people how to observe, interpret, apply, teach, and preach the Bible faithfully.

Artificial Intelligence serves this mission.

It never replaces it.

---

Artificial Intelligence is the assistant.

---

# The Seven Principles

## 1. Scripture First

The biblical text always has priority.

Every feature must lead users back to Scripture.

---

## 2. Observation Before Interpretation

Users should first discover what the text says.

Only afterwards should they consider what it means.

---

## 3. AI Is a Mentor

Artificial Intelligence asks questions before giving answers.

Its purpose is to develop the student's study skills.

---

## 4. Everything Is Explainable

Every suggestion made by BSMP should be traceable to a rule, observation, or biblical evidence.

The platform should never function as a black box.

---

## 5. The Student Owns the Study

Observations belong to the student.

Interpretations belong to the student.

Applications belong to the student.

The AI assists.

It does not replace.

---

## 6. Knowledge Is Structured

The inductive methodology is represented as structured knowledge.

The software consumes the knowledge.

The methodology is not hard-coded.

---

## 7. Ministry Before Technology

Technology exists to serve ministry.

Every feature should help people know God's Word more faithfully.

# The Role of Artificial Intelligence

AI never replaces Scripture.

AI never replaces the Holy Spirit.

AI never replaces careful Bible study.

AI assists by:

• Asking better questions

• Guiding observation

• Encouraging interpretation

• Stimulating application

• Organising research

• Assisting sermon preparation

while always encouraging users to examine the biblical text for themselves.

# Closing Statement

BSMP exists to glorify God by helping people understand His Word more faithfully and apply it more obediently.
